# Market and naming snapshot

Snapshot date: 2026-08-01. This is a positioning snapshot based only on the product sources already selected in the approved plan; it is not legal, trademark, domain, store, social-handle, pricing, or live popularity clearance.

The whitespace hypothesis to test is: **“a shovel-first competitive expedition where PvE mastery and objective timing can beat kill count.”** Research interviews and store creative should lead with the dig-to-discovery moment, then show the three-way endgame. “Battle royale” should appear only as a comparison qualifier, not the primary genre label.

## Working title decision

**Primary working title: Sandsunder**

- `Sand` communicates place and core material immediately.
- `sunder` implies breaking the ground, disrupting plans, and a decisive endgame.
- The wordmark can emphasize a shovel-cut fracture without borrowing genre iconography.
- Spoken form is compact in English and reasonably pronounceable internationally.

Recommended product descriptor: **Sandsunder — a competitive PvPvE expedition**.

Naming risks: the title does not by itself communicate top-down play or digging, and apparent uniqueness has not been verified. Do not purchase assets, announce publicly, create a Steam store page, or treat the title as cleared until exact and confusingly similar marks are searched in EUIPO, USPTO, UKIPO, WIPO, Steam, console stores, major PC storefronts, domains, and social handles by the responsible owner or counsel.

## Backup candidates for clearance failure

| Candidate | Strategic signal | Risk to validate |
|---|---|---|
| Shovelwake | Tool-first action and consequences left behind | May imply nautical movement rather than desert excavation. |
| Dustbound | Hostile desert journey and committed expedition | Less explicit about digging and may sound narrative-first. |
| Relic Rushdown | Objective convergence and fast combat | More generic and less ownable in tone. |
| Buried Crown | Treasure and a non-kill route to victory | Sounds more fantasy/narrative and less systemic. |

Do not run a public naming vote before clearance. Once two cleared finalists exist, test unaided recall, spelling after hearing, genre expectation, and logo legibility with the target Steam audience; select the title that best signals “desert excavation competition,” not merely the most-liked isolated word.
